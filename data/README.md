# Generated dataset area

`wexa-bench prepare` creates `raw/`, `normalized/`, `manifest.json`, and `query_bank.json` here.
Raw and normalized data are ignored; the small manifest and query bank may be committed after
their checksums and contents are reviewed.

`MOVIELENS_README.txt` is extracted byte-for-byte from the downloaded archive so the source's
usage license and citation guidance remain beside the reproducibility manifest.
